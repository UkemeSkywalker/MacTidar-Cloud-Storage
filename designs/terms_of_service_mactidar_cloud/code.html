Termination & Account Deletion

You are free to stop using MacTidar Cloud at any time. You may permanently delete your account, workspace credentials, and all associated raw storage objects directly through your Account Settings.

Prior to initiating account termination, we recommend using our asynchronous batch export tool to package your folders into an encrypted zip archive. Upon confirmation of deletion, our system irreversibly scrubs your encryption keys from our key vault within 48 hours.
